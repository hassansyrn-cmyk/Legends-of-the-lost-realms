Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the current Android game handles information and saved progress.

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Information handled by the game

The game does not require an account or sign-in. Gameplay progress and preferences are stored in app storage on your device. We do not receive that progress on our servers, and the game does not send personal information to us.

The current version does not include advertising, in-app purchases, or an in-game analytics service. We do not sell or share game data for advertising or analytics.

Android device backup

Android system backup is allowed for the app. If you have device backup enabled, Android may include eligible app data—which can include locally saved progress—in a backup associated with your Google Account and may restore it when you set up or reinstall the app. This backup is handled by Android and Google, not by our game servers, and we do not receive or control the backup copy. You can manage device backups in Android settings. For details, see Android Auto Backup.

Clearing the app’s storage removes the on-device copy. If a backup was created, manage or delete that copy through your Android/Google backup settings.

Retention and changes

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the game. This policy describes the current version; if the game’s data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date.
